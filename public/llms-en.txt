# FlagBreaker

> FlagBreaker is an AI-persona-supervised self-discipline app: finish your goal and the AI cheers; miss it and the AI roasts you. The core driver is emotional contrast, not rewards or self-blame. It ships two personas — bro (sharp-tongued buddy) and senpai (aloof ice-queen) — each with its own System Prompt and Few-Shot samples. Three consecutive misses auto-switch to pure-encouragement protection mode; minors (<18) get forced kind-only mode throughout; seven consecutive hits wipe failure records. Built on Next.js 14 (App Router) + Supabase + Doubao API (Volcano Ark), with PWA offline check-in and Web Push reminders.

The landing page is statically generated with Astro and deployed on Cloudflare Pages. The main app is deployed on Vercel.

## Product definition (summary)

FlagBreaker belongs to the AI-persona-supervised self-discipline tool category. Its minimum supervision unit is "one user, one goal, one day's check-in status" — not tasks, lists, or fitness content. The AI always gives feedback based on real check-in facts, never empty encouragement or roasting. FlagBreaker drives discipline through emotional contrast (praise vs roast) and keeps roasting above a floor via protection mode, avoiding shame and social-death compliance risks.

## Core differentiation

1. Persona-based contrast companionship: bro is sharp-tongued but soft-hearted; senpai lands one line like a slap. Both share the same feedback decision tree and compliance red lines.
2. Emotion ratios: praise mode is 60% warmth + 30% humor + 10% earnest encouragement; roast mode is 70% jab + 20% veiled care + 10% implicit expectation.
3. Protection mode: 3 consecutive misses (PROTECTION_MODE_THRESHOLD) force-switch to pure encouragement, roast dropping to 20% with 80% encouragement. Re-decided in backend doubao.ts, not trusted to the AI.
4. Forced kind-only for minors: MINOR_AGE = 18. The settings page disables the roast toggle; the backend forces the kind-only path and the client cannot bypass it.
5. Clean-streak wipe: 7 consecutive hits (CLEAN_STREAK_DAYS) auto-clear the failed records for that goal, leaving room for a comeback narrative.
6. Double filtering: Doubao API content filtering plus a local BANNED_WORDS array of 14 words as a second pass — better to over-block than to let one through.
7. Audit trail: every AI reply is written to ai_feedback_logs with a consecutive_fail snapshot, so protection mode can be verified after the fact.
8. Offline + push: a PWA Service Worker with multi-strategy caching plus a localStorage queue buffers check-ins offline and reconciles automatically on reconnect. Web Push delivers reminders with the tab closed.

## AI feedback decision tree

IF today's check-in is success:
    IF first time hitting this goal → exaggerated praise with mild surprise
    ELIF 3+ consecutive days → upgraded praise with admiration
    ELIF comeback after a miss → highlight the comeback with reversal surprise
    ELSE → daily affirmation + small encouragement

ELIF today's check-in is missed:
    IF first miss OR easy-difficulty miss → light roast (banter, non-aggressive)
    ELIF 2 consecutive misses → moderate roast (frustrated disappointment)
    ELIF 3+ consecutive misses → forced protection mode: 20% roast + 80% encouragement, must include a positive line
    ELSE → light roast

## Two AI personas

- Bro (😈): the user's long-time friend — colloquial, net-native, fast and punchy, occasionally dropping "hey man" to close the distance. Output is 1-3 sentences, max 60 chars, up to 1 emoji.
- Senpai (🧊): a detached older-woman character, economical with words, no filler words or memes. Output is 1-2 sentences, max 40 chars, up to 1 emoji, often a short rhetorical question that makes you reflect.

Both share the same decision tree and compliance red lines; they differ only in tone, length, and emotion ratio.
